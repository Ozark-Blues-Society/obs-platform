# Sponsors

## Where sponsors appear

- **The Sponsors page** (`/sponsors`, in the top menu): the featured sponsor's spotlight at the top,
  then every sponsor under its tier. Tiers come in their order, and sponsors by name within each
  tier. A "Become a sponsor" band at the bottom links to Membership Benefits & Join, because
  Sponsor is a membership level.
- **The home page**: "Our sponsors", with the same spotlight and every sponsor's logo, or the
  sponsor's name until its logo is uploaded. A link goes to the Sponsors page.
- **Pages** with a **Sponsor logos** block (see [Pages](pages.md)).

A sponsor shows in all of these as soon as it's published, and disappears from all of them, its
spotlight too, the day after its sponsorship ends.

## Sponsor tiers

Every sponsor belongs to a tier. The board plans three tiers but hasn't settled them yet, so for
now there is one, named **Sponsor**. If **Sponsor tiers** in the Studio is empty, create it first:

- **Tier name**: `Sponsor`. The Sponsors page lists the tier's sponsors under this name.
- **Price per year (dollars)**: `500`. The Sponsors page shows it next to the tier's name. Leave it
  empty to leave the price off.
- **Benefits**: one benefit per line. They show next to the tier's name too, so keep each one
  short.
- **Logo placement**: how this tier's sponsors appear on the Sponsors page: a large logo, a logo,
  or the name only. The home page shows every sponsor's logo at the same size, whatever its tier.
- **Order on the Sponsors page**: `1`. Tiers with lower numbers show first.

When the board settles the new tiers, add them here. No code changes are needed. A tier shows on
the Sponsors page only while it has a sponsor.

## Adding a sponsor

Open **Sponsors** and select **+** (Create).

- **Business name**, **Website** and **Logo** (a logo on a plain or transparent background works
  best; its alt text is usually the business name). The logo and the name link to the website.
- **Sponsorship level**: the sponsor's tier.
- **Featured sponsor**: shows the sponsor's spotlight on the home page and the Sponsors page.
  Only one sponsor can be featured at a time. To feature a different sponsor, first turn off
  **Featured sponsor** on the current one and publish it, then turn it on for the new one. A
  sponsor whose sponsorship has ended doesn't count. If two sponsors ever end up featured (the
  Studio's check can miss it when a sponsor sat open for a while before it was published), the
  website shows the one changed most recently. Turn off **Featured sponsor** on the other one and
  publish it.
- **Spotlight text**: a few sentences shown in the spotlight. A featured sponsor needs it.
- **Sponsorship ends**: optional. The day after this date (in Central Time) the sponsor disappears
  from the website by itself, so nobody has to remember to take the logo down.
