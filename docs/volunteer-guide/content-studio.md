# Content Studio

> **Stub.** This page covers the basics that work the same for everything you edit. Steps for each
> kind of content (pages, events, blog posts, venues, sponsors and site settings) are added as those
> parts of the Studio are built. Where something isn't decided yet, this page says so.

The Content Studio is where you change what the website shows. Open it by adding `/studio` to the
website's address, for example `https://<the Society's website>/studio`. It works on a phone, but a
computer is easier for longer edits.

## Signing in

The Studio has its own sign-in, separate from Society membership. An administrator invites you by
email. Accept the invitation, then sign in to the Studio with the same account you accepted it with
(Google, GitHub, or an email address and password).

## Drafts save themselves

Everything you type is saved straight away as a **draft**. Website visitors don't see drafts, so you
can stop halfway and come back later.

When it's ready, press **Publish**, and the change goes live on the website.

## Previewing your changes

Open **Presentation** at the top of the Studio to see the website, with your drafts, next to the
editing form. The preview updates as you type. Click a piece of text in the preview to jump to the
field it comes from.

## Sharing a preview

To show unpublished changes to someone who doesn't use the Studio, such as a band, a sponsor or a
board member:

1. In **Presentation**, open the page you want to show.
2. Select the share button above the preview (**Share this preview**).
3. Turn on **Share this preview with anyone who has the link**.
4. Select **Copy preview link**, then send the link to them.

The person who opens the link sees the website with all current drafts, not only that page, under a
yellow banner: "You're viewing a preview that includes unpublished changes". The banner's **Exit
preview** button takes them back to the published website. A preview never unlocks members-only
pages.

The link keeps working until sharing is turned off. To stop a link you've sent from working, turn
sharing off; turning it on again creates a new link.
