# Volunteer guide

How-to pages for the Society's volunteers who keep the website up to date: editors, the membership
manager, moderators and administrators. Plain steps, no technical background needed.

This guide grows with the website. It's finished during Launch 2 (decision 0002), and the two
recorded training sessions walk through it.

| Page                                                                                                  | For     | Status |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------ |
| [Content Studio](content-studio.md): signing in, drafts, who can see what, photos, previews and links | Editors | Draft  |
| [Pages](pages.md): templates, page blocks and menus                                                   | Editors | Draft  |
| [Events and venues](events-and-venues.md): events, repeats, venues and the venue directory            | Editors | Draft  |
| [Blog posts](blog-posts.md): writing, scheduling and the email digest                                 | Editors | Draft  |
| [Sponsors](sponsors.md): sponsors, the featured sponsor and sponsor tiers                             | Editors | Draft  |
| [People](people.md): authors and the board list                                                       | Editors | Draft  |
| [Site settings](site-settings.md): footer, social links, membership benefits and menu links           | Editors | Draft  |
| [Roles](roles.md): giving and removing volunteers' roles on the website | Administrators | Draft |
| [Members-only files](members-only-files.md): uploading files only members can download, and adding them to pages | Editors | Draft |

The pages are drafts: the Studio forms and admin screens they describe are built, and screenshots
come once the Society's accounts are set up. Still to come: the member list and recording payments,
and moderating classifieds.
