# Roles

Roles decide which volunteers can use the website's admin screens. Only administrators can give
and remove roles, on the **Roles** screen. Open it by adding `/admin/roles` to the website's
address, for example `https://<the Society's website>/admin/roles`, and sign in if the website
asks. Anyone else who opens it is told that only administrators can give and remove roles.

## What each role is for

- **Administrator**: gives and removes roles, this one included.
- **Membership Manager**: looks after member records: the member list, exports, and check or cash
  payments.
- **Moderator**: reviews classified listings before they go live.
- **Editor**: edits the website's content in the Content Studio, manages
  [members-only files](members-only-files.md) and makes the
  [monthly email digest](monthly-digest.md).

Someone can have more than one role. The screens for the member list and for classified listings
are still being built. For now the admin screens are Roles, Members-only files and Monthly digest.

**Editors also need a Content Studio sign-in.** The Studio has its own sign-in, set up in Sanity
(see [Content Studio](content-studio.md#signing-in)). Giving someone the Editor role on the website
doesn't do that for them.

## Giving someone a role

Everyone with a role needs an account on the website first, the same kind members have. Ask them
which email address they sign in with.

1. Under **Give someone a role**, type their email address.
2. Choose the role.
3. Select **Give role**.

The screen confirms it, for example "Dale Ramsey now has the Moderator role", and they appear under
**People with roles**. If no account uses that address, the screen says so: check the spelling, or
ask which address they use.

## Removing a role

Under **People with roles**, find the person and select **Remove** next to the role. The screen
confirms it, and someone left with no roles drops off the list. To give a role back, give it again.

## There's always an administrator

The website always keeps at least one administrator, so someone can always manage roles. It
refuses to remove the last one. To hand the role over:

1. Give the new person the Administrator role.
2. Remove your own.

Once your Administrator role is gone, you can't open the Roles screen any more.
