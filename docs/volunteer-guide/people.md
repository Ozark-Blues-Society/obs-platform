# People

**People** holds board members, volunteers and teams. Blog posts name them as authors, and board
members appear in the board list on the About page.

- **Name**: a person's name, or a team's, such as Communications Team.
- **Role**: optional, for example President or Treasurer.
- **Photo** and **Short bio**: optional. A head-and-shoulders photo works best; its alt text is
  usually the person's name.
- **On the board**: turn it on to list the person on the About page's board list, then set
  **Order on the board list** (lower numbers show first, so use 1 for the president).
