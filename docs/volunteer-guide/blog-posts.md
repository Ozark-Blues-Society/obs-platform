# Blog posts

Open **Blog posts** and select **+** (Create).

- **Headline**: the post's title, on blog cards and at the top of the post.
- **Web address**: press **Generate**. The post lives at `/blog/<web address>`.
- **Cover photo**: shown on blog cards and at the top of the post. Drag the circle onto the
  important part of the photo so it crops well on phones and cards.
- **Category**: Recap, Band Spotlight, Society News or Education. Readers can filter by category.
- **Author**: optional. Pick someone from **People** (see [People](people.md)), or a team such as
  Communications Team.
- **Short summary**: one or two sentences for blog cards and the monthly email digest.
- **Post**: write it in the Studio, or paste it from Word or Google Docs and the formatting comes
  along. Add photos between paragraphs; each photo needs alt text.
- **Who can see this post?**: see [Who can see it](content-studio.md#who-can-see-it). Visitors
  who can't read a members-only post see its headline and summary with a prompt to join.
- **Include in the monthly email digest**: turn it on to add the headline and summary to the next
  digest email.
- **When to publish**: the date the post shows, in Central Time. To schedule a post, choose a
  later date and time and press **Publish**: the post stays hidden until then.
