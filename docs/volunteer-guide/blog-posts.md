# Blog posts

Open **Blog posts** and select **+** (Create).

- **Headline**: the post's title, on blog cards and at the top of the post.
- **Web address**: press **Generate**. The post lives at `/blog/<web address>`.
- **Cover photo**: shown on blog cards and at the top of the post. Drag the circle onto the
  important part of the photo so it crops well on phones and cards. Everyone sees it, even on a
  members-only post, so pick one that's fine for the public.
- **Category**: Recap, Band Spotlight, Society News or Education. Readers can filter by category.
- **Author**: optional. Pick someone from **People** (see [People](people.md)), or a team such as
  Communications Team.
- **Short summary**: one or two sentences for blog cards and the monthly email digest.
- **Post**: write it in the Studio, or paste it from Word or Google Docs and the formatting comes
  along. Add photos between paragraphs; each photo needs alt text.
- **Who can see this post?**: see [Who can see it](content-studio.md#who-can-see-it). Visitors
  who can't read a members-only post see its headline, category, date and cover photo and a prompt
  to sign in or join, not its summary, the post, its author or its files.
- **Include in the monthly email digest**: turn it on to add the post to the digest for the month
  it's published in (see [Monthly email digest](monthly-digest.md)). The digest gives its headline
  and summary, or for a members-only post only its headline and a link to sign in and read it.
- **When to publish**: the date the post shows, in Central Time. To schedule a post, choose a
  later date and time and press **Publish**: the post stays hidden until then.

## On the site

- **The blog** (`/blog`) lists every post that's out, latest first, 12 to a page, with **Older
  posts** and **Newer posts** links at the bottom. Readers filter it with the category buttons
  (All, Recap, Band Spotlight, Society News, Education). Each filter has its own web address, such
  as `/blog?category=recap`, so you can share a link to one category.
- **A post's card** shows its cover photo, category, date, headline and short summary. A post with
  no cover photo gets a drawn stage picture instead. To someone who can't read a members-only
  post, its card shows the headline, category, date, cover photo and a **Members only** tag, with a
  blurred line where the summary would be.
- **The post's page** shows the headline, category and date, then the cover photo, the short
  summary, the author's name, the post and any files for members. If the author's entry in
  [People](people.md) has a photo or a short bio, a few lines about them end the post.
- **Scheduled posts** appear on the blog by themselves at the time you chose, with nothing more to
  press. Until then their web address shows "not found" and the monthly digest leaves them out. In
  the Studio's preview, and on a shared preview link, a scheduled post already shows on the blog,
  so you can check its card before it goes out.
