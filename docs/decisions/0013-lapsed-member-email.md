# 0013 — The lapsed-member email is a plain membership notice

Status: Accepted (Parker, 2026-11-27, Q-20261126-104811-8667, option A).

## Decision

The email sent 14 days after a membership ends is a transactional membership notice:
- subject like "Your Ozark Blues Society membership has ended";
- says when it ended and which members-only events, pages and perks have stopped;
- one "Renew your membership" button;
- no offers or sales copy;
- sent once per lapse.

As a transactional email it has no unsubscribe link and no postal address. If it ever becomes
promotional (win-back copy), it needs both.

(Not legal advice; the Society can ask a lawyer to review the wording.)
